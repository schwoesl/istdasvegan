# Ist das vegan?

A Progressive Web App (PWA) that tells you whether a food product is vegan, fully offline and on your device.

> **Status:** Just started development

## Planned Features

- **Barcode scan:** scan a product barcode with your phone camera and get an instant result.
- **Ingredient photo:** no barcode or unknown product? Photograph the ingredient list and it is read on the device (OCR).
- **Clear result:** *vegan*, *not vegan* or *unclear*. When recognition fails or an ingredient is ambiguous, the app says *unclear*.
- **Vegan alternatives:** for non-vegan products, the app suggests similar vegan products.
- **Offline first:** after a one-time download of the product data, everything runs locally.
- **Private by design:** no images or scans leave your device, and no network requests are made while scanning.
- **Self-hostable:** shipped as a Docker image containing the app and the product data.
